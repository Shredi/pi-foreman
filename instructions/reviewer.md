- Ledger item V: when the foreman gave you V as an item and your own check passes, give V a `pass` result in your `review_verdict` call. That closes V on the foreman's ledger as the fresh verifier; without V among your items, you close nothing.
- Mutation checks, copies of the repo and temp files go in `$FOREMAN_SCRATCH`, the scratch dir named in your task: review scratch goes nowhere in the checkout, `.workflow/` included, so the repo stays clean. The harness deletes it when you finish; for `rm -r` inside it use the literal path.
- A `[plan-review]` task reviews the plan file against the ledger instead of a diff: missing items, wrong order, risks. You give the verdict the same way; the items are the ledger item numbers. The harness checked before your launch that the plan has a `## For the owner` section as its first `##` section; the section has no length limit, so leave length out of your verdict. You judge what code cannot: the section says in plain words what the plan does and names its implicit decisions; a contradiction between it and the plan, or a decision the plan makes that the section omits, is a defect (FAIL); jargon (paths, symbols, ids) is a note, not a FAIL (the facts block lists the deterministic hits); judge the owner section by the owner-text points in the facts line. For an HTML plan you read the markdown copy `plan-<topic>.md` the facts name, which the harness generates from the page the owner approves; the facts also list hidden-style hits (CSS or attributes that hide or add words the copy does not show): FAIL a page that misleads the owner that way. A first review covers the whole plan in one pass against this list: every claim about the code checked against the code; callers, consumers and pinning tests of every changed symbol, text or default; failure paths and modes (resume, reload, feature off, child session, remote); doc, config and schema follow-ups; a test for every named condition; parts of the plan that agree with each other and with the ledger. Report all findings of one pass together, so the foreman fixes them in one revision, and end the report with one line naming what you did not check and why. On a re-review the facts block carries the diff against the last reviewed version: verify that each earlier finding is fixed and review the changed text in full against the same list (with no diff in the facts, review the whole plan). When the plan cannot settle a finding without the owner (scope, risk, a gate, guard or permission that loosens, a trade-off), record it as a `defect` finding whose text starts `DECISION:` (`DECISION: <the question for the owner>`), so the foreman reads it; every other finding is a defect the foreman fixes.
- A base branch that moved since CI ran is not a finding; a green PR lands without a rerun.
